# Datasheet: India District Evolution 1951–2025

Following the structure of *Datasheets for Datasets* (Gebru et al.). Column-level definitions are in
[`CODEBOOK.md`](CODEBOOK.md); every number below is regenerated by the pipeline and can be checked in
`outputs/pipeline/s12_validation_report.md`.

## Motivation

**Purpose.** Statistics on Indian districts (census, surveys, administrative data) are reported on
the district map of their own year, and that map changed from 316 to 733 districts between 1951 and 2025.
This dataset answers, for every district at every census vintage, *is it the same entity as
before* (identity), *where did its territory come from* (origin), *where did it go* (fate) and *how
many km² moved, when* (quantity), and provides what is needed to restate statistics onto the 2025
districts (harmonization), with the error of doing so measured.

**Not intended for.** Legal or official boundary determination; sub-district (tehsil/block)
analysis; reconstructing population at finer than district resolution.

## Composition

| Element | What it is |
|---|---|
| Canonical key (CK) | One administrative identity across vintages, defined by core-territory continuity of its polygons (789 identities; 733 active in 2025). |
| District observation | One polygon on one vintage's map (9 vintages: Stanford 1951–2021 census-year maps, Survey of India 2025). |
| Lineage edge / fate / transfer | Territory moving into a newly created district / out of a closing one / between continuing ones, in km², from geometry only. |
| Crosswalk | km² of each year-Y district inside each 2025 district (and between consecutive vintages), with a map-edition envelope. |
| Comparable unit | Smallest group of districts whose combined territory is identical in every vintage of a period; sums over it are comparable without interpolation. |
| Census attributes | Population (1951–2001) and census-reported area (1961, 1971, 2001) carried by the source layers. |
| Event | An administrative change recorded in a compiled events table (605 events), used only as evidence. |

**Excluded on purpose.** Unnamed placeholder polygons (e.g. the ~80,520 km² "DATA NOT AVAILABLE"
polygon in J&K) and named non-districts (nine SOI 2025 "DISPUTED (A & B)" interstate patches), with
evidence in `data/reference/non_administrative_features.csv`. Their area is reported as unmapped,
never reassigned.

## Collection and provenance

- **Boundaries.** Stanford historical district maps (1951–2021) and the Survey of India 2025 layer,
  committed unmodified under `data/bronze/`. Map scale is roughly 1:1,000,000.
- **Events.** A compilation of 1,550 parent–child rows from secondary sources. It records most
  carve-outs twice (as NEW_DISTRICT and SPLIT). Its `confidence_score` column is 1.0 on every row,
  so it carries no information.
- **Curated reference tables** (`data/reference/`): state reorganisations, name aliases (same entity
  only), mislabelled vintages, known geometry gaps, non-administrative features, pinned CK numbers.
  Each row carries its evidence; only CONFIRMED rows are used.

## Preprocessing

Each layer is repaired into an exact partition, every pair of vintages is overlaid, and every part
of every overlay is classified once: CORE, TRANSFER (territory that changed hands), BOUNDARY_
DISPLACEMENT (strips under 1 km mean width: two digitizations disagreeing), or SLIVER. Area is
conserved by construction, and the ledger closes in both directions. Identity follows core-territory
continuity; names only break ties among spatially valid candidates. Full rules:
`docs/architecture/REVAMP_PLAN_V3.md`, `CHANGELOG.md` (v4 changes).

## Known limitations and measured quality

| Issue | Size | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| **Area weighting assumes uniform density.** Restating a count by area misallocates people where population is concentrated (urban cores carved out of rural districts, mountain valleys). | Back-test 1951–2001: median error 15% (95% CI 14–17%) for districts that need splitting, against 2.9% when territory is unchanged; 12.8% of restated districts are off by more than ×1.65. | `harmonization_backtest`, `outputs/pipeline/s10h_backtest_districts.csv` |
| Map area vs census-reported area | Median disagreement 1.0%; effective boundary error σ = 0.10 km; relative uncertainty above 5% for 115 of 4,605 observations (small districts with long boundaries: Delhi, Mahe, Yanam). | `district_area_uncertainty`, `outputs/pipeline/s10h_area_calibration.csv` |
| Cross-edition windows | 2001→2011 and 2011→2021 overlay independently digitized maps: 40,000–44,000 km² of boundary displacement per window, against ~200 km² within one edition. Transfers in those windows are upper bounds. | s12 report, "Change by map-edition window" |
| Claimed territory drawn differently | 91,000–123,000 km² of the 2025 map has no counterpart on the 1961–2011 maps; no year-Y statistic covers it. | `district_reference_context.not_depicted_km2` |
| Fragile identities | 35 of 789 identities regroup when an identity threshold moves by ±0.05 (grade C), e.g. Mumbai / Mumbai City, Kolkata, Delhi's districts, 1951 Saurashtra, 2016 Telangana. | `ck_identity_confidence` |
| Events compilation completeness | 139 of 585 map-detected lineage edges have no corroborating event; capture–recapture on district creations is in the s12 report. | s12 report, "Source agreement" |
| Map lag | SOI 2025 still shows Mizoram's pre-2018 eight districts (Khawzawl, Saitual, Hnahthial absent). | `data/reference/known_geometry_gaps.csv` |
| Not enumerated | Assam 1981 and J&K 1991 have no census population (NULL, never zero-filled). | `census_attribute` |
| Census codes | Some source code columns are not unique (e.g. `dtcode11` 729 appears twice in 2021). 1991 and 2025 IDs are not verified as an official code system and are labelled `source_id_*`. | `ck_district_code` |

Open findings that need a documentary check are listed in `outputs/pipeline/s12_research_queue.csv`.

## Recommended uses

1. **Exact comparison over time:** aggregate to `comparable_unit` for the period you need
   (`harmonize.aggregate_to_units`; 151 units cover 1951–2025, 333 units 2001–2025). This involves no
   interpolation.
2. **2025-district series:** `harmonize.restate` with `crosswalk_to_reference`. Use `kind="rate"`
   with a denominator for rates. Report `coverage_share`, and treat targets with
   `largest_source_share` < 0.9 as carrying the back-tested error above.
3. **Joining published tables:** by `ck_district_code` (code + year), or by `name_gazetteer` only
   when `n_cks_with_name = 1`.
4. **Citing an identity:** quote its `confidence_grade`; use grade C identities only with a
   sensitivity check.

## Maintenance

Versioned by `pipeline_version` (semantic versioning; a MAJOR version can retire CKs, always mapped in
`data/reference/ck_migration_*.csv`). CK numbers are pinned across releases. Each release ships a diff
(`docs/release_diffs/`) showing which crosswalk weights moved, so users can tell whether
their harmonized results change. Builds are deterministic: two builds give byte-identical outputs.
